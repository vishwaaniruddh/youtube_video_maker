1. OVERVIEW
-----------
Chapter 4 covers converting the Hindi story script into dynamic, emotionally 
expressive voiceover audio using ElevenLabs and bracketed tone cues.

2. CORE WORKFLOW STEPS
----------------------
- Prompt Formatting in ChatGPT:
    "{GIVE ME ONLY SCRIPT FOR ELEVEN LABS WITH TONE VARIATIONS}"
- Output Tags: ChatGPT annotates the text with emotional directions:
    [Calm, Warm Tone]
    [Surprised, Fast Tone]
    [Confused Tone]
    [Confident, Heroic Tone]
    [Curious Investigative Tone]
- Platform: ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io) -> Text to Speech.
- Voice Choice: "Harsh - Resonant, Serious and Dramatic" (or suitable Indian voice).
- Model: Select "Eleven v3 (alpha)" - explicitly supports interpreting and performing 
  bracketed emotion cues without reading them aloud.
- Download: Generate and save the final master narration audio (MP3).

3. CONNECTION TO NEXT CHAPTER
-----------------------------
Now all media assets exist: motion video clips + voice narration. 
Chapter 5 demonstrates assembling, timing, and editing the complete cartoon in CapCut.
