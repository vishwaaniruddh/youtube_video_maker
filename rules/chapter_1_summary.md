## 1. Executive Summary

Chapter 1 introduces the foundation of the **Cartoon Video System**. Before generating images, voiceovers, or animations, you must establish the story script and its matching visual prompts. 

The chapter demonstrates how to use an AI chatbot (such as ChatGPT) to produce an end-to-end, scene-by-scene script paired with exact image generation prompts designed specifically for viral cartoon storytelling.

---

## 2. Core Rules & Constraints Taught in Chapter 1

1. **Single Narrator Structure:**
   - The script is structured for **1 narrator only**. 
   - No multi-character back-and-forth dialogue or voice acting needed; the single narrator tells the story and voices character expressions seamlessly.

2. **Strict Scene-by-Scene Architecture:**
   - The entire video is broken into distinct, numbered scenes (`Scene 1`, `Scene 2`, `Scene 3`, etc.).
   - **Rule of 1 Image Per Scene:** Each scene uses exactly one generated image or background visual.

3. **Two-Part Output per Scene:**
   Every scene output must contain two clearly separated elements:
   - **`IMAGE PROMPT`**: A vivid, descriptive English prompt detailing characters, mood, art style, setting, and lighting for the AI image generator.
   - **`SCENE SCRIPT`**: The exact narration lines (in Hindi or English) to be recorded for that scene.

4. **Target Duration:**
   - The tutorial recommends **3 minutes** as the optimal duration for high viewer retention and viral reach on YouTube.
   - The same prompt structure can easily scale to 5, 8, or 10 minutes simply by altering the duration parameter in the prompt.

## 3. The Master ChatGPT Prompt

Below is the exact prompt used in the tutorial:

```text
i am going to create a cartoon story video in hindi which only have 1 narrator so create a script for my 3 min story video. write scripts in scene because i am going to use 1 photo for each scene. no i mean first give me a prompt to generate the scene photo and just after that give the scene script
```

### Prompt Breakdown & Why It Works:
- **Format Directive:** *"write scripts in scene because i am going to use 1 photo for each scene"* ensures synchronized visual-to-audio mapping.
- **Order Directive:** *"first give me a prompt to generate the scene photo and just after that give the scene script"* ensures the image generator receives clean standalone prompts.
- **Voice Constraint:** *"which only have 1 narrator"* eliminates confusing multi-speaker requirements for TTS or voice actors.
