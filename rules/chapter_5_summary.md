## 3. Step-by-Step Editing Workflow

1. **Import Master Voiceover Track First:**
   - Always place the **ElevenLabs voiceover audio** on the timeline audio track first.
   - *Golden Rule:* The audio narration dictates the timing of every visual cut.

2. **Import Animated Video Clips:**
   - Import the animated MP4 clips generated in Chapter 3 (`Media 1`, `Media 2`, `Media 3`...).
   - Arrange them sequentially on the main video track.

3. **Beat-Matched Scene Synchronization:**
   - Listen to the voiceover and scrub the timeline to identify exact narrative transitions:
     - **Clip 1 (Village introduction):** Plays from `00:00` until *"laddus are missing..."*
     - **Clip 2 (Villager panic):** Cut cleanly when narrator says *"Achanak pure gaon mein shor mach gaya..."*
     - **Clip 3 (Golu investigates footprints):** Cut in when narrator says *"Gullu suraag dhoondhte huye talaab pahuncha..."*
     - **Clip 4 (Monkey in banyan tree):** Cut in when narrator says *"Vahan ek bandar baitha tha..."*
     - **Clip 5 (Magical glowing creature):** Cut in when narrator reveals *"Chamkeela sa jeev..."*

4. **Speed & Duration Control:**
   - Use the **Speed / Duration tool** (e.g., slowing clips to `0.3x` - `0.5x` with smooth frame blending) so each clip comfortably matches the entire spoken sentence without running out of frames.
   - Split and trim dead frames at sentence transitions.

5. **Channel Branding & Watermarking:**
   - Drag and drop your **Channel Logo** (e.g., "CW - Cartoon World") onto an overlay video/pip track.
   - Scale it down to a discreet size.
   - Position it in the **bottom-right corner**.
   - Extend the logo duration across the entire timeline from `00:00` to the end.

6. **Audio & Sound Balancing:**
   - (Optional) Layer subtle, cheerful royalty-free background music or ambient nature/village sound effects beneath the vocal track.
   - Ensure the voiceover volume is primary (`100%`) and background music is kept soft (`10% - 15%`).

7. **Export Settings:**
   - **Resolution:** 1080p (FHD, 1920×1080 for long-form landscape or 1080×1920 for Shorts).
   - **Frame Rate:** 30 fps or 60 fps.
   - Export to device storage.
