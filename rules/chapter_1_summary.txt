1. OVERVIEW
-----------
Chapter 1 covers the initial stage of creating cartoon story videos: generating 
the complete narrative script and pairing each scene with a dedicated AI image prompt.

2. CORE WORKFLOW RULES
----------------------
- Single Narrator: The script is written for a single voiceover narrator, 
  avoiding complex multi-character voice setups.
- Scene-by-Scene Layout: The video is divided into discrete scenes (Scene 1, 2, 3...).
- 1 Image Per Scene: Each scene corresponds to exactly one background image.
- Dual Output Structure: Each scene must output:
    a) IMAGE PROMPT: Detailed prompt in English (style, characters, lighting, environment).
    b) SCENE SCRIPT: Exact Hindi/English spoken narration lines.
- Duration: 3 minutes is recommended as the viral sweet spot, but can be scaled 
  to 5, 8, or 10 minutes.

3. THE MASTER CHATGPT PROMPT
-----------------------------
"i am going to create a cartoon story video in hindi which only have 1 narrator so 
create a script for my 3 min story video. write scripts in scene because i am going 
to use 1 photo for each scene. no i mean first give me a prompt to generate the scene 
photo and just after that give the scene script"

4. SAMPLE DEMOSTRATED SCENE
---------------------------
Story: "Gumshuda Topi Ka Mamla"

Scene 1: Ek Chota Sa Gaon Tha
IMAGE PROMPT: "Cartoon-style small Indian village in soft morning light, mud houses, kids playing, trees around, peaceful setting, bright cheerful colors."
SCENE SCRIPT: "एक छोटा सा गाँव था, बड़ा शांत और प्यारा। हर सुबह बच्चे खेलते, बड़े लोग काम में लग जाते। आज भी सब वैसा ही था... बस एक चीज अलग थी: आज किसी की टोपी गायब होने वाली थी।"

5. CONNECTION TO NEXT CHAPTER
-----------------------------
Chapter 1 outputs the structured text. Chapter 2 uses these image prompts to 
generate the actual visual assets via AI image generation tools.
