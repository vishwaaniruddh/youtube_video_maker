## 1. Executive Summary

Chapter 3 solves the static "slideshow" problem by animating each 16:9 cartoon image generated in Chapter 2 into a **smooth, moving video clip** using Meta AI’s free image-to-video animation engine.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow

1. **Access Meta AI:**
   - Search for **"Meta AI"** or navigate directly to `https://www.meta.ai`.
   - Sign in using your Meta (Facebook/Instagram) account.

2. **Upload Scene Image:**
   - In the prompt chat bar, click the `+` (Add media and files) icon.
   - Upload the 16:9 scene photo saved from Google Gemini (e.g., `Scene_01.png`).

3. **Trigger Video Animation:**
   - Click on the pre-built shortcut or type:
     ```text
     Create a video from my photo
     ```
   - Hit **Enter / Send**.

4. **Generation & Dynamics:**
   - Meta AI processes the still image and injects natural cartoon physics:
     - Characters gesture, look around, and blink.
     - Villagers and animals walk, mud water ripples, and foliage sways.
     - Lighting and depth effects create a 3D cartoon illusion.

5. **Download the Animated Clip:**
   - Once generation finishes, preview the video clip.
   - Click the menu / download button to save the animated `.mp4` file to your computer.

6. **Batch Process All Scenes:**
   - Repeat the upload and "Create a video from my photo" command for every scene in your script (`Scene 1` through `Scene N`).
