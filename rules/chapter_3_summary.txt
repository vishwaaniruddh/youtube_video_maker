1. OVERVIEW
-----------
Chapter 3 explains how to turn still 16:9 images into fluid moving animation clips 
for free using Meta AI's image-to-video capabilities.

2. CORE WORKFLOW STEPS
----------------------
- Tool: Meta AI (meta.ai).
- Upload: Click the "+" attachment button and select the scene's Gemini image.
- Command: Select or enter:
    "Create a video from my photo"
- Result: Meta AI adds realistic cartoon animation (character movements, eye blinks, 
  wind in trees, water ripples).
- Download: Save the generated MP4 clip to your system.
- Repeat: Perform this step for each scene in the storyboard.

3. KEY ADVANTAGE
----------------
This converts flat 2D cartoon drawings into living animated clips without expensive 
3D animation software or manual keyframing, completely free.

4. CONNECTION TO NEXT CHAPTER
-----------------------------
Now that all motion clips are collected, Chapter 4 focuses on generating voiceover 
narration with emotion and tone variations in ElevenLabs.
