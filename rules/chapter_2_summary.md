## 1. Executive Summary

Chapter 2 demonstrates how to convert the text-based image prompts created in Chapter 1 into full-color, high-resolution **16:9 cartoon scene images** using Google Gemini's AI image generation engine.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow

1. **Retrieve the Image Prompts from Chapter 1:**
   - Open your saved scene list (from Google Docs or ChatGPT).
   - Copy the English prompt under `IMAGE PROMPT:` for each scene.

2. **Access Google Gemini:**
   - Go to Google and navigate to `gemini.google.com`.
   - Log into your Google account.
   - Use the standard prompt bar or enable **Canvas / Image mode**.

3. **The Master Image Generation Command Prefix:**
   To guarantee the image generates in YouTube landscape widescreen format (16:9), add the exact prefix:
   ```text
   CREATE A 16:9 IMAGE - "<PASTE YOUR IMAGE PROMPT HERE>"
   ```

   **Example:**
   ```text
   CREATE A 16:9 IMAGE - "Cartoon-style small Indian village in soft morning light, mud houses, kids playing, trees around, peaceful setting, bright cheerful colors."
   ```

4. **Generation & Verification:**
   - The engine (powered by Google Imagen / Nano Banana) generates high-detail cartoon scenes.
   - Verify character consistency (e.g. detective Golu with magnifying glass, Indian villagers, monkey in tree).

5. **Download the Assets:**
   - Hover over the generated image and click the **Download** button.
   - Save the images organized by scene number (`Scene_01.png`, `Scene_02.png`, etc.).

6. **Alternative Options Mentioned:**
   - If you do not want to use Gemini, you can use **ChatGPT Plus (DALL-E 3)** or other free generative AI tools using the exact same prompt structure.
