1. OVERVIEW
-----------
Chapter 5 covers the final assembly process: importing the ElevenLabs audio and 
Meta AI motion video clips into an editor, synchronizing visual cuts to narrative 
dialogue beats, adjusting playback speed, and watermarking.

2. EDITING SUITES SUPPORTED
---------------------------
- VN Video Editor (demonstrated in video; free across mobile & desktop)
- CapCut (Desktop / Web / Mobile)
- KineMaster

3. STEP-BY-STEP ASSEMBLY RULES
------------------------------
1. Audio First: Lay down the ElevenLabs master narration track on the timeline first.
2. Sequence Clips: Add animated scene clips (Media 1 to Media N) on the video track.
3. Precise Dialogue Cuts:
   - Match scene transitions exactly to spoken narrative phrases 
     (e.g., transition to panic scene when narrator says "Achanak pure gaon mein...").
4. Speed Adjustment: Slow down short motion clips (0.3x - 0.5x speed) so the visual 
   lasts the full duration of its spoken narration beat.
5. Branding Watermark: Add channel logo in the bottom-right corner spanning 
   the entire video duration.
6. Audio Balancing: Keep narration dominant (100%), background music subtle (10-15%).
7. Export: 1080p, 30fps/60fps MP4.
