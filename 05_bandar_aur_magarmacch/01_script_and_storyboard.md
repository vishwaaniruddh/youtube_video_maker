# 🎬 Storyboard & Scene Breakdown: बंदर और मगरमच्छ (The Monkey and the Crocodile)
**Format:** YouTube Shorts (9:16 Vertical, 1080x1920)  
**Target Duration:** 36 Seconds (6 Shots × ~6.0s)  
**Style:** 3D Pixar Animation, vibrant lighting, expressive character faces, cinematic camera motion  
**Audio Style:** 100% Pure Hindi Narration (Priya - ElevenLabs Eleven v3), Zero character dialogue

---

## 🎭 Main Characters (Consistency Reference)
1. **Champak (बंदर):** A mischievous, intelligent, adorable 3D Pixar style golden-brown Indian monkey with bright curious hazel eyes, expressive animated face, fluffy fur.
2. **Makku (मगरमच्छ):** A large, friendly yet gullible emerald-green 3D cartoon crocodile with large expressive eyes, friendly rounded snout, textured scaly back.

## 📜 Full Hindi Narration Script (`audio/clean_script_shorts.txt`)
```text
नदी किनारे जामुन के पेड़ पर एक चतुर बंदर रहता था, जो रोज़ अपने दोस्त मगरमच्छ को मीठे जामुन खिलाता था।
मगरमच्छ की लालची पत्नी ने मीठे जामुन खाकर बंदर का कलेजा खाने की ज़िद ठान ली।
मगरमच्छ ने बंदर को दावत का बहाना बनाकर अपनी पीठ पर बिठाया और गहरे पानी में ले गया।
बीच नदी में जब मगरमच्छ ने सच बताया, तो चतुर बंदर ने मुस्कुराकर कहा—"अरे दोस्त! मेरा दिल तो पेड़ की डाल पर ही रह गया!"
मूर्ख मगरमच्छ बंदर को वापस किनारे लाया, और बंदर बिजली की फुर्ती से पेड़ पर छलांग लगाकर चढ़ गया!
चतुर बंदर ने हंसते हुए मूर्ख मगरमच्छ को भगा दिया। सच है, सूझबूझ और अक्ल से हर संकट टाला जा सकता है!
```

## 🎬 Shot-by-Shot Visual Prompts (Google Veo 9:16 Vertical)

### Shot 01: जामुन का पेड़ और दोस्ती
- **Scene:** Riverbank in a lush tropical forest, giant Jamun (blackberry) tree laden with deep purple fruits.
- **Visual:** 9:16 vertical 3D Pixar animation style. An adorable, fluffy golden-brown monkey with huge sparkling hazel eyes sitting on a low tree branch, cheerfully tossing plump purple jamun fruits into the open smiling mouth of a friendly cartoon crocodile floating in crystal river water. Sunlight filtering through lush green leaves, vibrant cheerful cartoon atmosphere, 8k render, living motion.

### Shot 02: मगरमच्छ की पत्नी का लालच
- **Scene:** Under river surface / muddy bank at sunset, crocodile's lair.
- **Visual:** 9:16 vertical 3D Pixar animation style. Deep inside the riverbank under glowing sunset lights, a vain and haughty female cartoon crocodile with an ornate flower behind her ear speaking menacingly, eyes gleaming with comical greed as she demands the sweet monkey's heart from the nervous, bewildered male crocodile. Expressive cartoon facial features, warm glowing river lights, dynamic camera angle, 8k render, living motion.

### Shot 03: पीठ पर सवारी व नदी का सफर
- **Scene:** Center of a sparkling wide river under bright blue sky.
- **Visual:** 9:16 vertical 3D Pixar animation style. Wide dynamic water action! The fluffy cute monkey sitting joyfully on the textured green back of the large crocodile swimming through the sparkling crystal blue river. Water ripples and splashes spreading outwards, tropical jungle in the background, monkey waving hands happily, 8k render, living motion.

### Shot 04: सच का खुलासा व बंदर की चालाकी
- **Scene:** Deep dark whirlpool river area, dramatic tension.
- **Visual:** 9:16 vertical 3D Pixar animation style. Dramatic close-up on the deep swirling river water. The crocodile looks guiltily over his shoulder, and the clever little monkey suddenly gasps in shock, then instantly taps his chin with a sly witty smile and points back toward the faraway shore. Cinematic water reflections, high expressive character acting, 8k render, living motion.

### Shot 05: पेड़ पर बिजली जैसी छलांग
- **Scene:** Riverbank shore right below the giant Jamun tree.
- **Visual:** 9:16 vertical 3D Pixar animation style. High-energy action shot! As soon as the crocodile touches the shallow river shore, the agile golden monkey takes a heroic dynamic flying leap from the crocodile's back, grabbing a thick mossy tree branch high above. Water droplets flying, extreme dynamic camera movement, triumphant cartoon leap, 8k render, living motion.

### Shot 06: सीख व फिनाले
- **Scene:** High branch of the Jamun tree, sunny morning finale.
- **Visual:** 9:16 vertical 3D Pixar animation style. Heartwarming and funny finale! High up on the sunlit Jamun tree branch, the safe and overjoyed monkey laughs hysterically, pelting ripe purple jamuns down at the embarrassed, baffled crocodile swimming away in shame. Golden warm sunlight, lush vibrant jungle, uplifting joyful cartoon ending, 8k render, living motion.
